# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Be respectful, assume good faith, accept constructive
feedback, and focus discussion on the work. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, and publication of private
information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and participation that violate these expectations. Report conduct
concerns privately to the project maintainers. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as
practical.
